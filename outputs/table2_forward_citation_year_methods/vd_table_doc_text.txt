Figure 1. Diagnostic analysis for the staggered DID Design
(a) Mean plots of granted patent applications per firm-year
(b) Dynamic ATT and event study plots
Note: Panel A shows observed and estimated counterfactual outcome trajectories while Panel B demonstrates Callaway–Sant’Anna event-study estimates. Panel A shows the mean annual number of ultimately granted patent applications for VD-backed and VC-only-backed firms relative to the year of first VD. The red dashed line represents the estimated counterfactual trajectory for VD-backed firms in the absence of VD, constructed using the Callaway and Sant’Anna (2021) event-time ATT estimates. Panel B reports the event-time ATT estimates, with vertical bars representing 95% confidence intervals. The horizontal line denotes zero treatment effect. Event time is defined relative to the year of first VD, with t=0 indicating the first VD year. Never-treated firms serve as the comparison group.
Figure 2. Heterogeneous event study estimates of the effects of VD on granted patent applications
(a) By prior technological assets
(a) By funding stage
Note: This figure presents heterogeneous event study estimates of the effects of VD on granted patent applications by prior patent status (Panel a) and funding stage (Panel b). Event time is relative to the first VD financing, with t= -1 as the omitted reference period. Bands indicate 95% confidence intervals based on firm-level clustered standard errors. 
Figure 3. Event study estimates across alternative estimators
Note: This figure shows event time estimates from a fully saturated event study specification using three alternative estimators. The treatment indicator, VD, is interacted with indicators for each year relative to a firm’s first VD indicator, with t = −1 serving as the omitted reference period. All specifications include firm and year fixed effects and the full set of time varying controls used in the main analysis. Never-treated firms serve as the comparison group. Bands indicate 95% confidence intervals constructed from firm-level clustered standard errors. The p-value from an F-test of joint significance on the original TWFE pre-trend estimates is 0.857.
