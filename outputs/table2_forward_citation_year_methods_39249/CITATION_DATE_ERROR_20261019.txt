Audit on 2026-10-19 identified a material construction error in
scripts/build_forward_citation_year_panel.py: g_us_patent_citation.citation_date
was used as the forward-citation event date. That field describes the cited
patent's grant month; it is not the date on which the citing patent was granted.
The post-grant filter and annual normalization therefore also used the wrong date.

Use the citing patent_id joined to g_patent.patent_date for event timing.

outputs/table4_window3_39249_20261019. The old DiD has not been rerun in that task.
