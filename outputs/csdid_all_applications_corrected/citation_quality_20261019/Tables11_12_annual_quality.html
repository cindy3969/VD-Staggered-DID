Interpretation: these DVs condition on having ultimately granted applications in the filing year. The treatment may itself affect that selection. All patent qualities use citations observed through 2024, including citations received after the filing year; they measure ex-post quality of that filing cohort, not citation receipts in that calendar year. Normalization does not eliminate unobserved future grant/citation selection. Original earlier DiD citation-date results are superseded for these outcomes.
