Annual firm-year patent-quality outcomes, added 2026-10-19

Requested change
The cross-sectional norm_cite_perpat_win3_v1-v4 definitions have been translated
to annual filing cohorts. New columns norm_cite_perpat_v1-v4 are LEVELS, not logs,
and do NOT repeat a three-year cross-sectional value across firm-years.

Patent universe and timing
39,249 unique firm-application observations filed 2010-2024, from the corrected
union after 737 mismatch removals. Normalization uses 34,855 grant-linked
applications, including zero-citation applications. The cohort population is
the study sample, not all US patents. Citation cutoff is 2024-12-31.
An incoming citation is dated using g_patent.patent_date of the CITING patent.
g_us_patent_citation.citation_date describes the CITED patent grant month and
must not be used for incoming-citation timing. The earlier annual DiD build
used that field incorrectly. All four new outcomes use corrected timing.
Unique application x citing-patent links are counted; 3 pre-grant links excluded.

Definitions (p=application, f=filing year, c=citation year)
v1_p = sum_c n_pc / mean(n_pc | f,c), with zero application-year cells included.
v2_p = sum_c n_pc / mean(n_pc | f,c,primary CPC3).
v3_p = rate_p,2024 / mean(rate_p,2024 | f), where rate is cumulative citations
       divided by 2024 - first_grant_year + 1. Use the earliest linked grant,
       including uncited grant numbers. Zero citations have zero rate.
v4_p = cumulative_citations_p / mean(cumulative_citations_p | f,primary CPC3).
Entirely zero normalization cells contribute zero.
For firm i and panel year y, each DV is mean(vk_p) over ultimately granted
applications filed by i in y. citation_quality_npat is this denominator.
If no eligible applications exist, the DV is missing. This preserves v1-v4;
the original zero-filled variants were named vv1-vv4 and were not requested.

Panel and code
The existing 88,547 rows, 7,273 firms and original 45 columns are preserved.
The 4 DVs plus citation_quality_npat are added to the existing DTA and CSV.
Original DTA and master do-file backups are in this directory.
MASTER_csdid_all_applications.do now calls RUN_ONE_QUALITY.do for versions 1-4
after its existing analysis. Each quality run reloads the full panel and applies
DV-specific complete-case filtering, without inheriting grantedapp_norm's filter.

Estimator and output
csdid method(dripw), same G/prior/early definitions, lagged cumulative funding,
lagged funding rounds, region and industry indicators as the existing master.
Table 11: all, no prior patent, prior patent, early stage, late stage; never-treated.
Table 12: all firms, never-treated and not-yet-treated controls; event window -4..4,
simple aggregate, post-event aggregate, and group aggregate.
Table 11 parentheses are p-values. Table 12 parentheses are standard errors.
Counts refer to complete-case inputs, not necessarily every ATT(g,t) pair.
Failed, omitted or zero-standard-error estimates are displayed as Not estimable.
The exact Stata return codes are retained in the detailed results.

Interpretation
The annual DV measures eventual observed quality of applications FILED in y,
using citations through 2024. It is not the flow of citations RECEIVED in y.
It uses future citation information relative to the filing year by design.
No-patent firm-years are excluded, so this conditions on patenting/grant selection
that may itself respond to treatment; it is not an unconditional innovation effect.
No-prior-patent treated firms may have no pre-treatment quality outcome, making
their ATT unidentified. Do not interpret omitted zero estimates as zero effects.
Normalization cannot fully recover future unobserved citations or pending grants.

Reproduction
1. scripts/build_table4_window3_corrected.py extracts corrected dated citation
   links and the application-year grid (its cross-sectional outputs are not
   used as the annual DiD outcomes).
2. scripts/add_annual_citation_quality.py creates annual_quality_addon.dta.
3. MERGE_ANNUAL_QUALITY.do adds those columns to the existing panel.
4. RUN_ONE_QUALITY.do 1 (and 2,3,4) estimates the two tables for each DV.
5. scripts/format_annual_quality_did.py produces the presentation tables.
