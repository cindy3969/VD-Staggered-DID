Table A6 reports treated and control means, standardized mean differences before and after matching, and variance ratios for continuous covariates. The Love plot provides a visual summary using an absolute-SMD benchmark of 0.10. These diagnostics assess balance on observed covariates and do not establish balance on unobserved determinants of venture-debt receipt.

Figure A1 displays the estimated propensity-score distributions before and after matching. Panel B shows improved overlap, although the distributions are not identical and perfect density overlap is not required. The figure documents overlap in estimated scores; it does not establish conditional independence, eliminate endogeneity, or prove that matching fully resolves comparability.
